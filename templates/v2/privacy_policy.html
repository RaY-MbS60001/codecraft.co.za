Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: November 2026

Introduction

CodeCraft Co ("we", "our", "us") runs this learnership and job-application platform. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you use it. By using the platform, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

What you give us

  • Your name and contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Your Google account profile (name, email, profile photo) when you sign in with Google
  • Your CV details, qualifications, work history and uploaded documents (CV, ID, certificates)

What we collect automatically

  • Basic device and browser information
  • How you use the platform (pages visited, applications sent)
  • Cookies used to keep you signed in and remember your preferences

How your applications actually work

When you apply to a company, we use your own connected Gmail account to send that company an email on your behalf, with your CV attached. We then read delivery and read receipts, and watch for a reply, so your application list can show a real status — Sent, Delivered, Read, Responded, Pending or Failed. We never see or store your Gmail password itself; access is granted and can be revoked through your Google account at any time.

How we use your information

  • To send your applications and track their status
  • To build and store your CV through the CV builder
  • To show you relevant learnerships and, for Premium users, suggested matches
  • To communicate with you about your account and applications
  • To keep the platform secure and stop abuse
  • To meet our legal obligations under South African law, including POPIA

Data security

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, Google OAuth for sign-in rather than storing Gmail passwords, and access controls on who at CodeCraft Co can reach learner data. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard steps to protect what you share with us.

Who we share your information with

  • The companies you choose to apply to — only once you send that application
  • Service providers who help us run the platform (hosting, email delivery)
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, if we're required to by law

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we never show your real contact details to other learners on public pages.

Your rights

Under POPIA and generally, you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your account and data
  • Withdraw consent (for example, disconnect Gmail access) at any time
  • Object to a particular use of your information

To exercise any of these, email privacy@codecraft.co.za. We'll respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember things like dark mode and dismissed banners, and understand how the platform is used. You can block cookies in your browser, but some parts of the site (like staying signed in) won't work without them.

Children's privacy

This platform is meant for people old enough to apply for a learnership (typically 16 and up). We don't knowingly collect information from children below that age.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know in the app or by email.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@codecraft.co.za, or use the contact form.
