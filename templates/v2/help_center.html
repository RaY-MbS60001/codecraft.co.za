Getting started

How do I create an account?

Either click Sign in with Google on the homepage and authorise access with your Google account, or register with your name, email and a password. Signing in with Google is the quicker route, since it's also what lets you send applications from your own Gmail later.

What is a learnership?

A learnership combines structured learning with real workplace experience, usually registered with a SETA (Sector Education and Training Authority). It typically pays a stipend while you learn, and gives you an industry-recognised qualification plus real work experience at the end.

Who can apply?

Eligibility varies by listing — always check the specific company's requirements — but most learnerships look for South African citizens or permanent residents, with at least a Grade 12 / Matric certificate, who are currently unemployed or looking to change direction.

Applications

How do I apply for a learnership?

Browse companies on the homepage, open the one you're interested in, and click Apply. We'll send that company an email from your own connected Gmail, with your CV attached. You can preview a company's profile first without leaving the page.

What documents do I need?

At minimum, a CV. Many learners also keep a certified ID copy, their Matric certificate and any qualification certificates in My documents, ready to attach when a listing asks for them. PDF, Word, JPG and PNG are all accepted.

How do I know if a company has seen my application?

Your Applications page shows a real status for each one: Sent, Delivered, Read and Responded (or Pending / Failed if something went wrong). This comes from your own Gmail account, so it's only as reliable as the read-receipt and delivery information Gmail can see.

Can I combine several documents into one PDF?

Yes — on My documents, tick two or more files and click Combine selected into one PDF. PDF, JPG and PNG can be merged; Word files can't be combined this way.

CV builder

What's the difference between the two CV builders?

CV Studio is the newer, template-based builder — pick a look, fill in your details, and it compiles a polished PDF with a live preview as you type. It also supports a photo. The classic builder is a simpler, faster option if you just want something basic and functional.

Can I see what a template looks like before choosing it?

Yes — each template in CV Studio shows a filled example so you can see roughly how your own CV will look once your details are in.

Account settings

How do I update my profile?

Open your profile picture in the top right and choose Edit profile. Update your details and save.

How do I reset my password?

From the sign-in page, use Forgot password and follow the emailed link. If you signed up with Google, there's no CodeCraft Co password to reset — manage access through your Google account instead.

How do I delete my account?

Email privacy@codecraft.co.za from your registered address and we'll delete your account and data. This is permanent and can't be undone.

Technical support

The site isn't loading properly — what do I try?

Clear your browser cache, try a different browser, check your connection, and disable any ad-blocking or privacy extensions that might be interfering. Still stuck? Contact us.

My document won't upload — what's wrong?

Usually it's file size or format. Keep files under a few MB, use PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG or PNG, and avoid unusual characters in the filename.

I'm not getting email notifications

Check your spam folder first, and make sure the email on your profile is correct. If you signed in with Google, also check that Gmail access is still connected.
