Code of Conduct

The ground rules for applying, reporting outcomes and talking to other users on CodeCraft Co.

Last updated: November 2026

Why this exists

CodeCraft Co works because applicants trust what other applicants post — honest reports of what happened after they applied. That only works if people play fair. This page sets out what we expect, and what happens if someone doesn't.

When you apply

  • Use your own, accurate CV and information — never someone else's
  • Don't apply to the same company over and over to spam an employer or inflate your numbers
  • Don't use the platform to send anything other than genuine learnership applications

When you post a report

After applying, you can tell other applicants what happened. When you do:

  • Report what actually happened to you — not a guess, and not someone else's experience
  • No links, email addresses or phone numbers in a report — they're stripped out and discouraged for everyone's safety
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats or personal attacks against a company, a staff member or another learner
  • No made-up or exaggerated outcomes meant to mislead other applicants

Anyone can flag a report that breaks these rules. We review flagged reports and may hide ones that don't belong, without warning the poster first.

Talking to other users

On the parts of the platform where replies or contact requests are visible to others, keep it respectful. We don't tolerate harassment, discrimination, or attempts to pressure someone into sharing contact details they haven't agreed to share.

What happens if you break these rules

Depending on how serious it is, we may hide the content in question, warn you, or suspend or remove your account. Serious or repeated abuse — fraud, harassment, fake reports used to damage a real company — can mean immediate removal without warning.

Report a problem

If you see content or behaviour that breaks this code, flag it where you see it, or email us directly at support@codecraft.co.za.

How this fits together

This Code of Conduct works alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If something here conflicts with the Terms, the Terms apply.
